## Decision: Blender toon models, posed per frame, layered body + head, shared palette
Options judged: (1) code-drawn vector (what we have; Lee rejected it); (2) layered 2D puppets with rotating parts: rotating pixel art destroys the pixels and needs hand-drawn parts that nobody (and no blind model) can make; (3) hand-pixelled frames: no artist, the Sparks cannot see; (4) Blender models rendered per pose then pixelated: same pipeline and light as the cars, any pose and any camera (the BACK view the original needs comes free), caricature proportions are numbers in a JSON, everything re-renders when the look changes. Chosen: (4), with two additions that make it work for faces and animation: the HEAD is a separate sprite layer (swap 16 faces for blink, talk and mood without re-rendering bodies) and every frame of one character is quantised with ONE shared palette (the car quantiser is per image, which would make an animation shimmer).
Proof (prototype, not committed): Mark built from spheres, capsules and a torus (curl-cluster hair, round glasses, navy jacket, raised open hand), cel-shaded with a 3-step ramp, rendered with Eevee at 1024, pixelated to 128 px with pixelate.py (28 colours, dither 0.18, outline). It read as pixel art: three clean tone bands, dither dots, a hard outline; the same model rendered from behind gave the back view. Four bugs found on the way, all fixed in the cards: (a) hex colours must be converted to LINEAR before Blender (tools/art/carlib.py does not: the car preview is washed out; the car colleagues should check); (b) a white world colour flattens every shadow, use (0.16,0.16,0.20) and sun 2.4; (c) the first hair had a bald patch at the back (GC05 covers the nape); (d) at 128 px a head is only 34 px: heads must be big (head_scale 1.3) and the frame tight.
Risk (be honest): the model builder is blind (text only). Poses are first-guess joint angles. Expect two or three QA rounds: render, look at `contact_sheet.py` PNGs (GC19), send number fixes ("shoulder_R ry -150 not -120"). Poses are JSON, so each fix is one number. Face likeness will need Lee's eyes.

## Units and sizes (all cards use these)
- 1 Blender unit = 1 metre; characters face -Y; front camera at -Y, back camera at +Y; L = world -X (screen-left in the front view).
- Art pixel = 2 logical px for world sprites and busts (recommend the same for all scenes). 64 art px = 1 unit.
- Body frame 144x144 (ortho 2.25; Mark centre z 1.17, Dave 0.95), head layer 80x80 (neck pivot at pixel 40,66), bust 96x96 (single) or 144x96 (group customers). Drawn at integer scale 2 (3 on title/end screens).
- Lighting: warm key from upper left front (sun 2.4), cool rim (#a8c4ff, 0.6); 3-step toon ramp 0.58/0.86/1.06; palette per character in docs/characters/*.json (<= 32 colours + outline #14102a); ordered dither 0.18.
- Mark: navy suit #243a66/#1b2c50, shirt #f4e9c8, tie #7a1f2b, skin #f1c9a5, hair #7a5430/#5a3b24/#a07848, glasses #15151d. Dave: overalls #2f5d9e/#223f70, bib #3a6fb5, cap #4a4a52, moustache #6b4a2e, skin #e8bf98, nose #d98a78, mug #e8e0cc.

## Poses, faces, timings (data: docs/characters/poses_*.json, expressions.json)
Faces (16): neutral blink happy grin laugh worried panic shock smug sad angry think + talk_A/E/O/M. Mouths 11. Head angles 7 + back. Blink every 3.4-4.6 s for 0.14 s (per-character seed). Talk: 14 letters per second drive the mouth (a e i o u m b p letters pick the shape); body gesture clip rotates talk_a/b/c per sentence; a line lasts at most 4 s.
Mark front (at desk): idle 6f@3fps, tap 4f@8, talk_a/b/c 6f@8, phone 4f@4, read 4f@2; one-shots shrug 5f@8, point 4f@8, thumbs 4f@8, facepalm 5f@8, slump 4f@6; loops panic 6f@12, celebrate 6f@10. Mark back (seated, watching the telly): idle_back, laugh_back, scratch_back, point_back, lean_back (22 frames). Dave front: idle, sip (mug up and down), talk, shrug, point (spanner), thumbs, panic, facepalm, celebrate (50 frames). Dave back on the stool: idle_back, laugh_back, sip_back. One-shots hold their last frame (0.6-1.0 s) then return to idle automatically.
Busts (96x96, heavy frame + name plaque, drawn by banner.js): Mark 8 faces, Dave 6 faces, 24 customer archetypes (16 jobs + 8 extras) x 2 colour variants = 48 busts (single or 2-3 person groups; no gendered pronouns in text, varied inclusive comic looks: veil and top hat couple, beret photographer, rock band with mohawk, stag party, hen party, mayor, vicar, panto dame, chef, extras: pigeon fancier, knight, beekeeper, clown, lollipop person, ghost, robot, family). Portrait choice is a hash of the customer name (never the game rng).
